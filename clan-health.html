How the score works

Transparent · Season 4 onward
Participation · 35%

Members with a recorded positive gain in the latest seven completed days, divided by the tracked roster.

Activity momentum · 25%

Average of the change in weekly reputation gains and the change in active-member count versus the prior seven days.

Contribution balance · 20%

Rewards a broader share of gains; measures the top 20% of active contributors against an even share.

Member retention · 20%

Share of members active in the previous seven-day period who were also active in the latest period.

Risk thresholds: Healthy 70–100, Watch 45–69, High risk 0–44. Major risk indicators can raise the displayed risk level. The score stays hidden until 15 consecutive daily ranking snapshots are available; the first day’s gain needs the prior cumulative snapshot.

Dates use Asia/Manila. The current implementation covers the Chaos roster because other clans do not yet have the same durable member-level daily history.
