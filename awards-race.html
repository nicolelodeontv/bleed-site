How the race is calculated

👑 Season MVPHighest total reputation gained in the active season.
⚔️ Most ActiveMost unique Manila calendar days with a recorded increase.
🔥 ConsistencyLongest consecutive streak of calendar days with a recorded increase.
🎯 Recorded IncreasesMost positive changes detected between shared game reads.
📈 Most ImprovedLargest positive change in reputation gained per calendar day versus the previous tracked season. Returning members with positive gains in both seasons qualify.
🌱 Rookie of the SeasonHighest-gaining member with no saved member row in the previous tracked season.

The standings are provisional and can change after each data sync. Final trophies are calculated and permanently recorded when the season closes. Members with no positive metric are not ranked. Most Improved and Rookie stay unavailable until a previous tracked season exists.
