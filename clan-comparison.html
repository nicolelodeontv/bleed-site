What this comparison means

Observed rankings only

The tracker saves one full clan-ranking snapshot per Manila calendar day. Reputation change is the difference between two saved cumulative totals; rank improvement is the earlier rank minus the latest rank, so positive means a climb. Member change compares the roster count shown by the ranking source.

A comparison is shown only when the exact baseline date exists. Missing days are not backfilled, and this page does not estimate individual member activity or assign health scores to clans without member-level history.
