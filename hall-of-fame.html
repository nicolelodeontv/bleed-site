Award definitions

👑 Season MVP
Highest total reputation gained during the season. Tied leaders all receive the trophy.
📈 Most Improved
Largest positive increase in reputation gained per calendar day compared with the member's immediately preceding tracked season. Only returning members with positive gains in both seasons qualify.
🌱 Rookie of the Season
Highest season gain among members first present in this season's saved member baseline. Appears only when a preceding season exists.
⚔️ Most Active
Most unique Manila calendar days with a recorded reputation increase.
🔥 Consistency Award
Longest consecutive streak of calendar days with a recorded increase. Streak tracking starts with the durable streak ledger; earlier streaks are not reconstructed.
🎯 Most Recorded Increases
Highest number of positive changes detected between shared game reads. These are recorded increases, not guaranteed individual fights.
All tied winners receive the award. Members with no positive recorded metric do not receive an award. Most Improved uses season-duration-adjusted daily gain rates; Rookie requires a previous season baseline. All trophies are frozen once a season closes.
